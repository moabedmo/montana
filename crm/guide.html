📱 Part One: Sales Rep Guide

1Signing In and Installing the App

  • Open this link in your mobile browser: montana2.vercel.app/crm
  • Choose the "Sales Rep" tab, then enter the email and password you received from the admin.
  • To install the app on your phone's home screen: from the browser menu, choose "Add to Home Screen".
TipOnce installed, the app works just like any regular app — it opens faster and supports working offline.

2Building the Week Plan (Saturday → Thursday)

Each rep prepares a week plan and submits it for admin approval. Planning days are Saturday through Thursday — Friday is off.

  • From the home screen, tap "Create Plan".
  • You can plan one day or more — you don’t need to fill every day.
  • For each day you include: choose one or more active clients in your territory plus AM or PM.
  • Submit for admin approval. Once approved, you can write visit reports from the plan.
NoteDoctors the admin has set to "Inactive" won't appear for you — only Active doctors show in the list.
ImportantWithout an approved plan, you won't be able to submit a visit report. Submit your plan early in the week.

3Setting Today's Status

From the home screen, tap "Set today's status" and choose the day type: regular working day, Saturday, annual leave, office day, or sick day. Your annual leave balance appears on the same screen.

4Logging a Visit (Field Report)

  1. From the "This Week's Visits" list, or from the days in your locked plan — tap the visit / doctor.
  2. Four steps: Visit type → Location → Products/Samples → Notes and submit.
  3. Location: recorded from where you write the report. The allowed range is 200 meters from the client's address. A client's first visit without saved coordinates automatically records the clinic's location.
  4. Period: AM or PM — pulled from the plan, and you can change it.
  5. Visit type: actual visit, regular, or no show.
  6. Products: choose the products discussed during the visit.
  7. Samples: set the quantity with the + and − buttons from your allocation. Under each product: "You have: X units". If you enter more than your allocation, the system still records it but flags it for the admin.
  8. Competitors / Photo (optional), then write your notes and submit the report.
Important — GPSIf you're more than 200 meters from the client's address, you can still submit the report after confirming — the visit gets ⚠ Flagged and a warning reaches the admin in "GPS Audit" and Telegram. Final verification happens on the server.
Creating an Invoice for a Pharmacy/DoctorIn the notes step, enable "Deliver products to pharmacy". When you pick a product, it won't show every company product — only what you actually have in your allocation, with the number of remaining units next to each one. Set the quantity (original price before any discount), payment method (cash / partial / on credit), and discount percentage if any. When you submit the visit, the invoice is created immediately and deducted from your allocation right away — no need to wait for the admin. You can only invoice a doctor or pharmacy that's in your bricks.

If the discount is over 40%: the invoice won't be created immediately — it will be sent to the admin for approval, and you'll get a confirmation message once approved.
Invoice for a Client Not Registered in the SystemThe "🧾 Invoice" button at the top of the screen (works from anywhere in the app) opens a quick form: enter the client's/pharmacy's name (plus phone and address if you like), add the products, and set payment and discount just like any regular invoice. There's no requirement that they be in your bricks or even registered in the system at all — useful when you're giving products to a pharmacy or doctor who hasn't been added yet.

5Working Offline

  • If the internet drops during a visit, log it as usual — it will be saved on your phone, and an orange banner will appear at the top.
  • As soon as the connection returns, visits sync automatically, or tap the "⟳ Sync" button at the top.
  • No need to worry about duplicates — the system prevents the same visit from being logged twice.

6Other Screens

  • History: all your past visits with location verification status, filterable by type.
  • Materials: promotional materials for products — show them to the doctor from the screen during the visit.
  • Ranking: your standing among your colleagues based on plan coverage and sample distribution.
  • Profile: your details, and a "🔔 Enable daily reminders" button to turn on a daily morning notification with your remaining visit count — enable it once and you're set.

🖥️ Part Two: Admin Guide

Sign in from the same link montana2.vercel.app/crm, choosing the "Admin" tab. Using a computer is recommended.

1Adding Doctors and Pharmacies (System Foundation)

Reps build their schedules from the data you add here — so this is the first step:

  • Manual entry: from the "Doctors & HCOs" page, tap "+ Add doctor" and fill in: name, class (AB1 through BB2), specialty, type (doctor / pharmacy / polyclinic / hospital), territory ("Brick"), address, and phone.
  • Visits per month: the "Visits / month" field lets you set how many times this doctor or pharmacy should be visited per month. If left blank, the system uses the default based on class (AB1=4, AB2=3, BB1=2, BB2=1). Any number you enter here applies immediately in the rep's schedule, even if the schedule was already created.
  • Bulk import: the "↑ Import Excel" button accepts Excel and CSV files — download the ready-made template from the same window; columns accept headers in Arabic or English. Territory is matched by name automatically.
  • Editing: the "Edit" button next to any doctor opens all their data for editing, and you can change the visit count at any time.
  • Approval: if a rep adds a new doctor, it appears as "Pending" and you must approve it before it shows up for other reps.
  • Active / Inactive: the status column in the doctors table — Inactive hides the doctor from reps and excludes them from the weekly plan. Use the filter by brick or status.

2Managing Rep Accounts

  • From the "Sales Reps" page, tap "+ Add rep" — the account activates immediately without email confirmation.
  • The "Manage" button lets you edit data, deactivate the account, send a password reset link, or permanently delete it (which also removes the login account itself).

3Reviewing Locations — "GPS Audit"

  • Any visit logged more than 200 meters from the clinic's address appears here, with the distance in meters.
  • If the circumstance is justified (e.g., the doctor changed clinics), tap "Approve" to approve it manually.
  • Reps cannot approve their own visits — that permission is admin-only.

4Territory Map

  • All doctors appear on the map, with each point colored by its class.
  • Enable "Show flagged visits" to display rep locations at the time of flagged visits, compared against the clinic's location.

Rep InvoicesWhen a rep creates an invoice for a pharmacy/doctor from their app, it appears immediately in the "All invoices" table on the Sales & Invoices page — with the "Source" column set to Rep, and the "Rep" column showing who created it. You can view the amount paid and remaining for each invoice individually.

Manually linking an invoice to a doctor/pharmacy: if an invoice has no linked client (the "🔗 Link" button appears in the last column of the table) — for example, imported invoices, or invoices a rep created for an unregistered client — click the button, search by name, and select the correct doctor/pharmacy from the database.
Approving Discounts Over 40%Reps are allowed to discount up to 40%, and the invoice is created immediately. If a larger discount is requested, you'll receive a Telegram message with all the details and two buttons, ✅ Approve / ❌ Reject — the invoice is only actually created after you approve it.
Overdue InvoicesEvery day at around 9 AM, you'll receive a Telegram message listing every invoice whose due date has passed and hasn't been fully collected yet.
Reconciliation FilesAt the bottom of the Sales & Invoices page, the "↑ Upload file" button lets you upload Excel/CSV files (e.g., statements from distributors) for storage and future reference — the file is stored and you can download it again whenever you need it; it is not read or parsed automatically.
Sample AllocationsFrom the same "Samples Report" page, you can give each rep a quantity of samples from the warehouse (an "allocation") for them to draw from as they log visits, instead of deducting directly from store inventory. Choose the rep, product, and quantity, then tap "Give / reclaim samples" — a negative number returns the quantity to the warehouse. 6Products and Promotional Materials

  • Products: add and edit company products (name, description, active/discontinued).
  • eDetailing Materials: upload PDF files or images for each product — they appear immediately in the "Materials" screen for all reps.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

My location won't lock in, or it says "Could not get GPS location"?

Turn on location services in your phone's settings, allow the browser to access your location, and try to be in an open area — then tap the doctor again.

I logged a visit while offline — did it get lost?

No, it was saved on your phone. As soon as the connection returns it will sync automatically, or tap "⟳ Sync".

A rep forgot their password?

The admin goes to "Sales Reps" → "Manage" → "Reset password" — the rep will receive an email with a link to set a new password.

A new doctor isn't in the system?

The rep should notify the admin to add them. Once approved and set to Active, they'll appear in the week plan.

I locked my plan by mistake — can I edit it?

Not from the rep side. Contact the admin. The weekly plan can only be locked once.

Why is a visit flagged even though the limit is 200m?

Make sure the database server is updated to the 200m limit (migration 081). Otherwise the system may still be calculating with an older limit.

Morning notifications aren't arriving?

Make sure you enabled them from the "Profile" screen, and that notifications are allowed for the browser in your phone's settings. They're sent daily at 6 AM.

A visit is flagged ⚠ even though the rep was actually at the clinic?

The clinic's saved location is likely outdated or inaccurate. The admin can approve the visit from "GPS Audit", and correct the clinic's coordinates by editing the doctor.
